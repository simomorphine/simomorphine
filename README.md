<h2 align="center">🧠 Humble Systems Theory</h2>

<p align="center">
My broader research direction is <strong>Humble Systems Theory (HST)</strong> — a framework exploring Information Processing Systems (IPS) as systems that receive information, pay computational costs, pursue goals, operate under constraints, and affect future states.
</p>

<p align="center">
One of the mathematical objects emerging from this work is the complex-valued quantity
</p>

<p align="center">
<strong>Q(sᵢ, sⱼ) = cost(sᵢ, sⱼ) + i · debt(sᵢ, sⱼ)</strong>
</p>

<p align="center">
The complex structure is intended to represent more than simply adding another numerical dimension. It is part of an attempt to investigate whether reinforcement learning can be formulated using a richer mathematical representation of cost, debt, state transitions, and system dynamics.
</p>
